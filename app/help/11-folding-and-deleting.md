# Folding and deleting

Hide the steps you are not looking at, and remove tasks for good.

## How it works

**Fold.** A task with subtasks gets a **▾**. Click it and everything underneath collapses into that one line. The line still shows the rolled-up time, the deadline, the progress bar, and a note when the "next up" task is one of the hidden ones.

Folds are saved per task, so the list comes back in the shape you left it, on every device. Anything that puts a task inside a folded one (the **+** box, a breakdown, a drag) opens it back up.

**Delete.** Every task has a **✕** at the end of its row, finished ones included. It removes the task from the database, along with everything nested under it.

- A task with nothing under it asks once.
- A task with subtasks tells you how many ("has 7 subtasks nested under it, and they all go too"), names the first few, and puts the total on the button.

**Discard** (🗑 in the task dialog) is different: it keeps the task and marks it dropped, so it moves to the *Done, dropped & missed* section.

## Why it works this way

A task with subtasks is one line by design, and may well be folded shut. That makes what you are about to lose invisible at the moment you click ✕, so the confirmation spells it out instead of asking a bare "are you sure".

A fold opens itself when something lands inside it so nothing ever ends up somewhere you cannot see.

For a [repeating task](19-repeating-tasks.md), deleting one copy skips that copy only. The confirmation says so, and tells you that **Repeat → Doesn't repeat** is how to end the series.
