# Projects and tabs

Each tab across the top is a project: one list of tasks. Work, house, that side thing.

## How it works

- **Switch** by clicking a tab, or with **Alt+1** to **Alt+9**.
- **＋** starts a new project.
- **Click the open tab** to rename it.
- **✕** on a tab deletes the project and everything in it.
- **Drag a tab** along the strip to move it. On a touchscreen, hold and slide. From the keyboard, focus a tab and use **Shift+←** / **Shift+→**. **←** / **→** on their own just move along the strip.

Adding, braindumping, breaking down, ordering and focusing all act on the tab you are on and leave the others alone. The app reopens on the tab you were last in, and the tab order you set is the same on every device.

To move a task to another project, open it and pick a new **Project** in its dialog. Its subtasks go with it.

## Why it works this way

Tabs are views over one page rather than separate places to navigate to. Switching is one click and changes nothing except which list is on screen, so there is no "where was I" after switching.

Reordering never switches tabs, because moving a tab and opening it are different intentions and mixing them makes the strip jump under your cursor.

**Transition alarms ignore tabs on purpose.** A cue you miss because its task is one tab over is exactly what this app exists to prevent, so alarms fire wherever you are and say which project they came from. See [Transition alarms](21-transition-alarms.md).
