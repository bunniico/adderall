# Sorting and ordering

Read your list however you need to, or arrange it by hand.

## How it works

**Sort.** The control above the list orders it by:

- **Smart** (the default): urgency first, quick wins ahead of slogs.
- **Manual**: exactly as you dragged it.
- **Score**: the app's [one-number answer](14-the-score.md) to "what deserves the next hour".
- **Deadline**, **Subtasks** (open steps, counted all the way down) or **Created**.

The arrow button beside it flips the direction. Tasks the field says nothing about (no deadline, say) sink to the bottom either way.

**Drag.** Grab a task by its **⠿** handle and drop it:

- on the top or bottom edge of another task to sit above or below it,
- in the middle of another task to nest inside it,
- on empty list space to pull it back to the top level.

It works with a mouse, a touchscreen, or the keyboard: focus a handle and use **↑** / **↓** to move, **→** to nest under the task above, **←** to pop out.

## Why it works this way

**Sorting is a lens, not a change of plan.** Steps inside a task keep the order the breakdown gave them, and ▶ Focus still hands you the most urgent thing. Reading your list by deadline for a minute never quietly changes what you are about to work on.

**The first drag switches to Manual** and freezes whatever order you were looking at, so a list sorted by deadline keeps that order and only the task you moved moves. From then on the list, including which task is "next up", stays as you arranged it.

**Nesting is the exception.** Which task something belongs inside is true however the list is read, so dropping one task into another leaves the sort alone.

Picking another Sort turns Manual back off. The *Manual order* switch in ⚙ Settings is the same switch.
