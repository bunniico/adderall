# The All tab

Once you have more than one project, an **All** tab appears at the head of the strip. It shows every list compiled into one, ordered by urgency, with each task labelled with the project it came from.

## How it works

Everything you can do to a task works from here: tick it off, open it, break it down, focus on it, delete it. While All is open, **next up** and **▶ Focus** pick across every project, so the task wearing the "next up" badge is the task Focus will hand you.

A few things stand down on this tab:

- **The add box** is hidden. A compiled list has nowhere to put a new task.
- **Dragging** is off. The compiled list has no order of its own to drag into.
- **Manual** disappears from the Sort control.

The project you were on before opening All is still the one that adding and braindumping act on, and it is where you go back to when you want to arrange things.

## Why it works this way

All holds nothing itself. It is a way of reading your lists, so anything that would need a single list to act on (adding, dragging) belongs to the real tabs.

Manual order is dropped because where you dragged something in one tab says nothing about where it sits among another tab's tasks. Mixing two hand-made orders would produce an order nobody made.
