# Habits

The **Habits** tab holds routines: the work that never finishes. Meds, a walk, ten minutes of journalling.

## How it works

Click **＋ New routine** and answer three questions:

1. **What is it?**
2. **Which part of your life?** 🏠 Life, 💊 Health, 🏃 Exercise or 🧠 Mentality.
3. **How often?** Every day, on certain days of the week, or a number of days a week.

Tick the big circle when you have done it today. That is the whole daily interaction.

Under each routine is **a year of squares**, one per day. Filled means you did it, faint means it was owed and missed, empty means that day never asked for it. **Click any square** to fill it in or clear it.

Next to it are two numbers:

- **The streak** counts only the days the routine names. A weekly target's streak is counted in weeks.
- **The 30-day rate** is the share of its chances the routine actually got in the last month.

## Why it works this way

**A routine is not a task.** It has no estimate, priority or deadline, because none of those make sense for something that is never done. So routines live in no project and never reach the task list, the calendar, ▶ Focus or your day cap.

**"A number of days a week" exists** because "run three times a week" is a real habit, and pinning it to named days makes it a lie.

**Squares are editable** because a day you forgot to tick is still a day you did it, and a mistake you could not undo would make the whole calendar something you stop trusting.

**Streaks are forgiving by design.** A Sunday the routine never claimed cannot break a streak, and today not being ticked *yet* never breaks one either; the day is not over. A display that says you failed every morning is a display you stop opening. The 30-day rate sits next to it to keep a long streak in proportion.
