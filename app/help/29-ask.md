# Ask

**💬** in the bottom-right corner opens a chat with the app itself.

## How it works

Ask how anything works: "why did this land on Thursday?", "what does the score mean?", "how do I make a task repeat?". It answers from these Help articles.

Or ask it to do something: "add call the dentist tomorrow", "mark the laundry done", "what should I do next?". It works your list with the same tools an outside AI agent gets from the [MCP server](26-other-apps-and-agents.md): listing, adding, editing, starting, completing and deleting tasks, compiling a braindump, and checking off habits. Each reply shows a small chip for every action it took, and the list redraws straight away.

**New chat** clears the conversation. Nothing is saved: the conversation lives in the page and is gone when you close or reload it.

## Why it works this way

**It only acts when you ask.** A question gets an answer, not a change. Before deleting something or changing a lot of tasks at once, it says what it is about to do and waits for you to confirm.

**It knows what Help knows.** The articles are written to explain how each feature works and why, so they are what the chat reads from. If they do not cover something, it says so rather than guessing.

**Same tools as any other agent.** It cannot do anything the MCP server cannot, and every change it makes lands exactly the way a click would.

**It counts against your [AI budget](23-ai-and-your-budget.md)** like every other AI call, on the balanced model, stepping down as the day's spend climbs. Turn it off with the *Ask* [module](27-modules.md).
