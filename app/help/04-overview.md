# Overview

The **Overview** tab is the first one in the strip. It reads your lists rather than being one.

## How it works

**Tiles across the top** show open tasks, what is overdue, what is due in the next seven days, how much work is left, how much of today is already booked, how much you finished in the last month, and your level and daily XP.

**Finished work** is a chart of the last 30 days: one bar per day, minutes above and tasks below, with a line for your [day cap](17-planning-your-days.md). Days where you finished nothing are drawn rather than skipped.

**Next up** lists the most urgent task on each list, in the order [▶ Focus](20-focus-mode.md) would hand them to you. **Coming up** lists the nearest deadlines across every list. Each row says which tab it came from, and clicking one opens it right there.

At the bottom, **Work left by list** and **Work left by category** show where the remaining work sits: per project, and per corner of the [impact/effort matrix](13-impact-and-effort.md).

Unlike [All](03-the-all-tab.md), the Overview is there even with a single project.

## Why it works this way

A list can tell you what is on it. It cannot tell you how much there is altogether, what is about to land on you, or whether you are getting anywhere. The Overview answers those.

Nothing on it is calculated separately. It is built from the same numbers the list and the calendar use, so "4h 30m booked today" is the same four and a half hours the calendar draws, not a second opinion.

Quiet days are shown because a chart that skips them makes a patchy month look like a busy one. The charts are drawn by the app itself with no outside libraries, so the page works offline.
