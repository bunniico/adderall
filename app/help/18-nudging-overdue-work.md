# Nudging overdue work

Move a missed deadline forward in one click.

## How it works

The red **overdue ⏩** badge on a task is a button. Click it, in the list or on the calendar, and pick **in 1 hour**, **tonight**, **tomorrow, same time**, **in 3 days**, **next week**, or a moment of your own.

The task keeps its length: the estimate never changes, so it takes up as much of the new day as it did of the old one. For a task with subtasks, the whole plan slides by the same amount, and its start time moves with its deadline.

On the calendar, everything overdue is gathered into one rail with a **Nudge all** button, for when the pile is the problem. It asks where the app should start looking, previews where things will land, and offers **These aren't happening** to drop the lot.

## Why it works this way

A deadline that has already passed is the most demoralising thing a list like this can show you, and retyping a date for every item is exactly the friction that leaves it showing. So the badge that tells you is also the fix.

Keeping the length means three days of steps stay three days of steps instead of collapsing onto the new date. Moving the start time along means a plan pushed to next week keeps its shape at both ends, instead of claiming it should have begun last Tuesday.

Dropping a pile is offered alongside moving it because sometimes the honest answer is that those things are not happening.
