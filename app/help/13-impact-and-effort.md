# Impact and effort

Every task is rated for how much it matters and how much it takes.

## How it works

Each task carries an **impact** and an **effort** rating from 0 to 10. The AI suggests them (turn this off with *AI impact/effort suggestions* in ⚙ Settings) and you can move the sliders in the task dialog.

Together they place the task in one of four categories:

- ⚡ **Quick wins**: high impact, low effort.
- 🏔 **Major projects**: high impact, high effort.
- · **Fill-ins**: low impact, low effort.
- 😮‍💨 **Thankless**: low impact, high effort.

The *Impact/effort threshold* in ⚙ Settings sets where "high" starts.

When a task comes out **thankless**, the app gently suggests dropping it. You can discard it, keep it anyway, or ask for a re-score.

## Why it works this way

This is the action-priority matrix, combined with the Eisenhower idea that urgency and importance are different things. It gives a quick answer to "is this worth my energy" without having to think it through each time.

Thankless tasks eat energy and give little back. They are usually best minimised, delegated or dropped, and the prompt exists because giving yourself permission to drop something is hard to do alone. It is a suggestion, never an automatic action.

Impact and effort also feed the [score](14-the-score.md), and the calendar can filter by category.
