# XP and levels

Finishing a task pays out its score as XP.

## How it works

A task worth ★ 62 earns 62 XP. Your level and a progress bar sit in the top-right corner. When XP lands, the bar slides and `+62 XP` floats up under it; passing a level runs the bar out to full before starting the next one.

Levels get steadily further apart: 100 XP to reach level 2, another 200 to reach level 3, and so on.

The side rail shows two averages:

- **Average hourly XP**, across every estimated task that has ever paid out.
- **Average daily XP** over the last 30 days, counting quiet days. The Overview repeats this one as a tile.

The Done list keeps what each task paid. The whole thing, confetti included, is one switch in ⚙ Settings: *Gamification*.

## Why it works this way

**One number, not two.** XP is the score, so there is nothing extra to learn and nothing to farm. The way to earn more is to do the work that was worth more.

**XP already earned stays earned.** A task that has paid once never pays again, however often it is reopened, and deleting it later costs you nothing. Losing points for tidying up your list would punish exactly the wrong thing.

**Containers pay through their steps**, never twice for the same afternoon's work.

**The daily average counts quiet days** so it shows the pace the month really ran at, not what a good afternoon pays.
