# Braindump

**🧠 Braindump** empties your head into one box and turns it into tasks.

## How it works

Type everything that is swirling around, in any order, as messily as you like, and press **Compile into tasks**. The Compiler turns it into separate tasks in the project you are on.

Steps that belong to one outcome are **nested** under it as subtasks, up to three levels deep. Unrelated one-off things stay at the top level.

Every new task is then estimated and scored like any other.

## Why it works this way

Getting things out of your head is the hard part, and sorting them at the same time makes it harder. Braindump separates the two: you dump, the app sorts.

Nesting is there so a braindump comes back as a few projects with steps rather than a flat wall of thirty items, which is its own kind of overwhelming.

It uses the most capable model with extended thinking, in a single call, because grouping a messy paragraph correctly is the hardest language job the app does and it only happens once per dump. If you have set a [daily budget](23-ai-and-your-budget.md), it is the first thing to move to a cheaper model as the day's spend climbs.

If the internet drops mid-dump, the request is queued and runs when the connection is back. See [When the connection drops](25-when-the-connection-drops.md).
