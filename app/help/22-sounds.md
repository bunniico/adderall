# Sounds

A soft click on every press, and a chime when something lands.

## How it works

In ⚙ Settings → **🔉 Sounds**, turn *Sound on* and set the volume.

You can replace any of the six sounds with your own audio file (mp3, wav, ogg, or anything the browser can play, up to 2 MB each):

- **Button press**: every press, everywhere.
- **Hover**: pointing at a button.
- **Task finished**: checking something off.
- **Level up**: passing a level.
- **XP gain**: the XP bar filling up.
- **Alarm**: a transition alarm.

## Why it works this way

Feedback you can hear makes an action feel finished, which helps when it is hard to tell whether something registered.

The built-in sounds are generated by the browser on the spot, so there is nothing to download and the first click makes a noise straight away.

Custom sounds are stored in this browser only and never uploaded. That keeps them private, and it means they do not follow you to another device.

Sound and motion are both optional. With sound off, or with your system set to reduce motion, nothing in the app stops working.
